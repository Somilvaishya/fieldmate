FieldMate · Testing manual

0 se sab tak — pura app test karne ka tareeka

Site khaali karne se le kar rep ka pura din, distributor ka portal, paisa, season booking aur manager ki screens — sab kuch, usi kram me jisme test karna chahiye.

Ye doc kaise padhein. Har kaam pe likha hai wo kahan karna hai:

terminal aap chalaoge   Desk browser me, manager ki tarah   Phone app me, rep ki tarah

Jahan "Ye hona chahiye" likha hai — wahi asli test hai. Agar wo na ho, to wo bug hai, aage na badhein; neeche Bug kaise likhein dekh lein.

Part 0 — Zero se site taiyaar karna

Ye hissa ek baar ka hai aur aap karoge, rep nahi. Iske bina phone pe kuch nahi dikhega — aur khaali site bilkul toote hue app jaisi lagti hai, is liye ise poora khatam karein tab rep ko phone dein.

0.1 — Kaunsi site, aur test flag terminal

Do site rakhein: ek test site jo mitayi ja sakti hai, aur ek pilot site jo asli data ke liye hai. Saare script test flag maangte hain aur uske bina chalte hi nahi. 

bench --site <test-site> set-config fieldmate_test_site 1

Ye flag pilot ya production site pe kabhi na lagayein. Ye flag hi wo cheez hai jo reset_fieldmate.py ko asli data mitane se rokti hai. Flag laga diya, to wo rakawat khatam.

0.2 — App install aur migrate terminal

bench get-app https://github.com/Somilvaishya/fieldmate --branch <branch>
bench --site <site> install-app fieldmate
bench --site <site> migrate

ERPNext zaroori hai. HRMS (check-in aur expense ke liye) aur India Compliance (GST, e-way bill) bhi install hone chahiye. Scheduler chaalu rakhein, warna KPI aur reminder ke job nahi chalenge.

Ye hona chahiye

bench migrate bina error khatam ho. Lekin migrate clean chalna iska matlab nahi ki kuch kaam karta hai — wo aage test karenge.

0.3 — Sab mita ke sach me zero pe aayein terminal

Agar site pe pehle ki testing ka kooda pada hai, to pehle dekho kitna hai:

cd sites
../env/bin/python ../apps/fieldmate/scripts/reset_fieldmate.py            # sirf ginti
../env/bin/python ../apps/fieldmate/scripts/reset_fieldmate.py --yes      # sach me mitao
../env/bin/python ../apps/fieldmate/scripts/reset_fieldmate.py --yes --keep-masters

Bina --yes ye sirf ginti karta hai. Pehle aise hi dekhein. --yes permanently mitata hai, undo nahi hai.

Ye kabhi nahi chhuta: Company, accounts, warehouse, UOM, Item Group, aur wo Customer / Item / Item Price jo FieldMate ne nahi banaye. Doosre app (crm, trako waghairah) ko bhi nahi.

0.4 — Company, GST aur Item Tax Template Desk

  1. Company banayein — GSTIN aur poora address bharein.
  2. Tax account aur Sales Taxes and Charges Template banayein (In-State / Out-State).
  3. Jo item bechne hain, unke Item Group pe Item Tax Template lagayein.

Company ka GSTIN zaroor bharein — ye sabse zyada chhoota hua kaam hai.

GSTIN na ho to India Compliance place of supply tay nahi kar paata, koi GST template choose nahi hota, aur phir: order me GST dikhta hai par invoice bina GST ka jaata hai. Rep ₹3,360 bolega aur bill ₹3,000 ka banega. E-way bill bhi nahi banega. Template ka rate bhi check karein — "GST 12%" naam ka template jiska rate zero ho, har jagah zero padhta hai aur bug jaisa lagta hai.

0.5 — FieldMate Settings Desk

Desk me FieldMate Settings kholein aur bharein:

  • Default company
  • Kaunsi sale chaalu hai — primary aur secondary (dono chaalu rakhein)
  • Credit ka bartaav, visit aur geo-fence ke figures, kaam ka din
  • MRP price list ka naam
  • Satchel alert kitne din baad (cash in hand)

0.6 — Territory ka ped Desk

State aur area ka tree banayein. Rep, party aur catalogue — sab isi pe tangte hain, is liye ye galat hua to aage sab galat dikhega.

Territory khaali na chhodein. Frappe khaali Link field me session ka default bhar deta hai, aur tab ek master jo "sirf Delhi North" ke liye tha, root node pe chala jaata hai — yaani har jagah. Yahi cheez chup-chaap galat catalogue aur galat credit limit dikha deti hai.

0.7 — Price list Desk

Alag-alag ERPNext Price List banayein, aur unme item price load karein:

ListKiske liye
PTD / PTSSDistributor ya super stockist humse kis rate pe khareedta hai
PTRDistributor apni shop ko kis rate pe bechta hai
MRPSettings me naam daalein. Isse upar wali line order me jaane se rukti hai

Selling Settings me "Selling Price List" ka naam zaroor bharein. Jahan ye khaali hota hai, wahan "jo list pehle mil jaaye" uthayi jaati hai — aur ek extra selling list banne par jawab badal jaata hai. Isse pehle 89 test ek baar fail hue the aur message bas "in ka price nahi hai" bolta tha, wajah chhupa deta tha.

0.8 — Item, catalogue aur must-sell Desk

  1. Item banayein — HSN code (6 ya 8 digit), stock item, sales item.
  2. Har item ka price kam se kam PTR aur PTD list pe daalein.
  3. FieldMate Product Catalogue banayein — territory aur channel ke hisaab se.
  4. Must Sell List banayein (optional) — jo har shop me rakhwana hai.

Ye hona chahiye

Catalogue ka har item kisi price list pe hona chahiye. Jiska price nahi hai, wo phone pe "no price yet" dikhega — ye sahi bartaav hai (zero book karna ya andaaza lagana isse bura hai), par rep ko ye toota hua lagta hai. Rep ko phone dene se pehle ye zero karein.

0.9 — Distributor Desk

Distributor ek ERPNext Customer hai, aur uske alawa kuch nahi. Koi alag distributor record nahi hai. Har super stockist / distributor / wholesaler pe bharein:

  • ERPNext ka apna: credit limit, payment terms, default price list, territory, GSTIN, address
  • FieldMate ka: Channel Tier, parent customer, humse ya parent se khareedta hai, shops ke liye PTR price list, warehouse, assigned rep

Warehouse zaroor bharein. Season allocation stock kahin reserve karta hai, aur wo "kahin" yahi warehouse hai. Khaali chhoda to ERPNext item master se apna warehouse chun lega, aur phir aap stock ek warehouse me rakhoge jab Sales Order doosra naam leta hai — allocation kuch bhi reserve nahi karega aur wajah dikhegi nahi.

0.16 — Phone pe app Phone

  1. Chrome me kholein: https://<site>/fieldmate
  2. Menu se Add to Home screen / Install karein.
  3. Wi-Fi pe ek baar login karein, taki pehla pura data utar jaaye.

Pehla login Wi-Fi pe hi karein. Pehli baar me shop, catalogue, price aur scheme sab phone pe utarta hai. Ye adhoora raha to rep ko aage khaali screen milegi.

Part 1 — Rep ka pura din (phone)

Yahi asli product hai. Neeche ka sab kuch rep ki tarah karein, phone pe. App me paanch tab hain: Today · Shops · Orders · Collect · Me.

1.1 — Login Phone

Rep ke apne login se sign in karein. Phone pehchana jaata hai, to doosre phone se wahi login karne pe app ko pata chalta hai.

1.2 — Day start

  1. Today tab → Start day.
  2. App location maangega. Dein.

Ye hona chahiye

  • Day start hone ke baad Today pe "Day running" dikhta hai.
  • Niche Today's route aur aaj ke shop dikhte hain.
  • Chhutti wale din Holiday ya "You are on leave" dikhta hai — khaali screen nahi.

1.3 — Shop me check-in

  1. Route me se ek shop pe tap karein → check in.
  2. Shop ke bahar khade ho kar bhi try karein.

Ye hona chahiye

  • Geo-fence ke bahar se "Outside range" dikhe.
  • Ek visit khula rehte hue doosri shop me check-in karne par app roke aur us shop pe wapas jaane ka raasta de — chup-chaap pehli visit band na kare. (Ye ek asli bug tha: pehle wo visit band ho jaati thi, chhoti stamp ho jaati thi, aur kisi ko pata nahi chalta tha.)

1.4 — Visit ke andar ke kaam

Visit screen pe "What to do here" ke neeche ye milenge:

KaamKya check karein
OrderNiche 1.5 me poora
ChecklistSawaal us shop ke liye sahi aayein; jawab save ho
Shelf checkPhoto khinche aur bina signal bhi lag jaaye
Stock checkPichhli ginti saath aaye, taki "pichhli baar jitna" ho sake
Collect paymentShop ka outstanding dikhe (Part 3)
PhotosShelf, shop front, planogram, competitor, damage, expiry

Ye hona chahiye

Photo record ke baad aati hai, apni line me. Signal jaane par bhi photo phone pe pakka rakhi jaati hai aur baad me chali jaati hai.

1.5 — Shop ka order (secondary sale)

  1. Visit → Order. Catalogue khulta hai.
  2. Item daalein. Cart kholein.
  3. Schemes screen dekhein — kaunsa offer laga, kya chhoot gaya.
  4. Review → shopkeeper ko order padh kar sunayein → tick karein → Send.

Ye hona chahiye

  • Cart me GST ki line aur bill total dono dikhein. Niche ke button pe bill total ho, sirf goods ka nahi — rep jo number bolega wahi shaam ko invoice pe aayega.
  • MRP se upar wali line order me na jaaye — uska naam aur MRP dikhe.
  • Review ke neeche saaf likha ho ki order distributor ki queue me jaa raha hai, wahi deliver aur bill karega. Yahan se koi invoice nahi banta.
  • Credit limit se upar jaane par order phir bhi chala jaaye — breach dikhe aur manager ke paas jaaye. Counter pe rep ko roka na jaaye.
  • Desk me dekhein: shop ke order ka koi Sales Order aur koi GL Entry nahi bana.

1.6 — Bina signal (sabse zaroori test)

  1. Signal ke saath cart banayein.
  2. Phone ka data band karein (aeroplane mode).
  3. Quantity badlein. Screen band kar ke phir kholein. Order send karein.
  4. Collection bhi lein, photo bhi khinchein — sab offline.
  5. Ab data chaalu karein aur intezaar karein.

Ye hona chahiye

  • Quantity badalne pe koi spinner nahi, koi network nahi — total turant.
  • Order "saved on this phone" bole aur outbox me jaaye.
  • Signal aane par sab apne aap chala jaaye. Ek bhi record do baar na bane.
  • Phone jo total bola aur server ka total — paise tak same.

Yahi app ka asli imtihaan hai. Rep ka pura 9 ghante ka din bina signal chale aur ek bhi record na khoye — yahi poora design iske liye bana hai.

1.7 — Check out aur Day end

  1. Visit → finish. Agar order nahi liya to reason maanga jaaye.
  2. Din ke aakhir me Today → Finish day.

Ye hona chahiye

Day end pe distance, kitni call, strike rate — sab khud banein, rep type na kare.

1.8 — Me tab

  • My performance — target aur kitna hua, incentive ka andaaza
  • Expense claim — distance server ka, claim rep ka, farak manager ka

Part 2 — Distributor pe order (primary sale)

Ye shop ka order nahi hai. Ye wo sale hai jo hum karte hain aur hum bill karte hain. Is liye iska asar seedha books pe padta hai.

  1. Orders tab → Order on a distributor.
  2. Party chunein. Catalogue khulta hai.
  3. Cart banayein → Review → Send.

Ye hona chahiye

  • Catalogue party ki apni price list pe ho, kisi shop ki nahi.
  • Review pe likha ho ki ye hum invoice karte hain — "distributor shop ko bill karega" na likha ho. (Ye line pehle har order pe aati thi, jo galat thi.)
  • Button pe "Send to the office" likha ho, "Send to distributor" nahi.
  • Desk me: ek Sales Order bana, usi party ke naam.
  • Dobara sync hone par doosra Sales Order na bane.
  • Party ki limit se upar wala order roka jaaye. Chhodne wale sirf ye ho sakte hain: FieldMate Admin, Sales Head, System Manager; ya rep ka apna manager (Reports To ki chain pe, to area manager chhutti pe ho to regional kar sakta hai); ya wo depot jiske paas order gaya. Khud rep kabhi nahi, aur doosre area ka manager bhi nahi — teen territory door wale banda ka is shop ke paise se koi lena-dena nahi.

Catalogue, cart aur review — teeno pe ek hi rate dikhna chahiye. Aur Desk me Sales Order ka rate bhi wahi. Agar catalogue ka rate aur book hua rate kisi round percentage se alag hai, to ye wo bug hai jo season me milta tha — turant likhein.

Part 3 — Paisa: collection se ledger tak

Koi bhi field role ledger me kuch nahi daalta. Rep collection likhta hai; Payment Entry tab banta hai jab office deposit confirm karta hai. Yahi niyam finance ko is system pe sign karne layak banata hai — is liye ise theek se test karein.

3.1 — Shop se paisa Phone

  1. Visit → Collect payment, ya Collect tab.
  2. Outstanding bill dikhein. Amount daalein, mode chunein, receipt banayein.

Ye hona chahiye

Shop ka paisa distributor ka hai. Isse koi Payment Entry aur koi GL Entry nahi banta — kabhi nahi, deposit confirm hone ke baad bhi nahi.

3.2 — Depot se paisa Phone

  1. Collect tab → Take from a depot.
  2. Depot, amount, mode. Cheque ho to cheque ki date bhi.
  3. Booking ke against ho to wo chunein (Part 4).

Ye hona chahiye

Booking chunne par depot apne aap us booking ka ho jaaye. Kyunki ek depot ka paisa doosri party ki booking pe lagana — galat account me credit aur kisi aur ka order release.

3.3 — Deposit, aur tab ledger PhoneDesk

  1. Phone: Collect → satchel → Hand in the money. Kya-kya de rahe hain tick karein, slip number daalein, declare karein.
  2. Desk: doosra banda (rep nahi) deposit confirm kare.

Ye hona chahiye

  • Declare karne ke baad bhi Payment Entry nahi bana — screen saaf bole ki office confirm karega.
  • Confirm hone par sirf us paise ka Payment Entry bane jo humara tha.
  • Jisne declare kiya wahi confirm na kar sake.
  • Cheque bounce karein → Payment Entry cancel ho aur invoice ka outstanding wapas aaye.

3.4 — Post-dated cheque (PDC)

Aage ki date ka cheque lein, phir deposit screen kholein.

Ye hona chahiye

  • Us row pe "Dated … — not bankable yet" likha ho.
  • Wo apne alag block me ho — "Post-dated cheques" — aam cheque ke saath na ho.
  • Upar Post-dated ka column ho, jo cheque ke total ka hissa ho, uske upar nahi.
  • Likha ho ki office use date tak rakhta hai aur koi booking release nahi hoti.
  • Phir bhi wo hand-in karne layak ho — rep use satchel me rakh le to kisi ke paas nahi pahunchega.

Part 4 — Season booking (Holi)

Holi ka stock November me book hota hai aur February me jaata hai. Is liye season order ek aam order nahi — uska rate pakka ho jaata hai, advance chahiye, aur line-wise delivery date hoti hai.

Pehle seeding chalayein:

cd sites
../env/bin/python ../apps/fieldmate/scripts/setup_phase2_season.py

Iske bina season ki screens khaali dikhengi — aur khaali screen toote hue app jaisi lagti hai.

Iska poora test alag doc me hai — docs/MANUAL_TESTS_PHASE2_SEASON.md, 11 section. Yahan sirf chhota sa daur:

  1. Distributor pe order banayein. Review pe "Booking · <season>" ka card dikhna chahiye — rate pakka, kitna advance, delivery window.
  2. Line ki delivery date window ke bahar karein → button grey ho aur saari galat line ka naam dikhe.
  3. Send karein. Order Awaiting Advance me ho aur koi Sales Order na bane.
  4. Aage ki date ka cheque dein → booking phir bhi ruki rahe.
  5. Cash me advance dein → Sales Order bane, aur uska payment schedule season ke terms se aaye.
  6. Orders → Season book: kitna chahiye, kitna aaya, kitna baaki — aur PDC alag dikhe, advance me juda hua nahi.
  7. Desk → Season Allocation: teeno rule ka preview, apply, revert.
  8. Desk → ⋯ menu: deliver, invoice. Invoice pe advance laga hua ho aur outstanding utna kam ho.
  9. Desk → Season Cockpit: har figure ginti me sahi ho.
  10. Orders → Share the range: link banayein, usse private window me kholein, bhejein, aur switch off karein.

Part 6 — Distributor ka portal

Distributor ke login se (rep ke nahi) — setup_phase9.py do login banata hai.

  1. /distributor/orders — apna order queue. Accept, invoice, deliver.
  2. /distributor/sales — apni sales ki CSV file bhejein.

Ye hona chahiye

  • File ka result dono number bole — kitni line padhi aur kitni nahi, row number aur wajah ke saath. 347 me se 3 kharab ho to 344 jayein aur wo 3 naam se dikhein.
  • Phone pe us shop ka outstanding ab distributor ke invoice se bane, hamare order se nahi. Dono ek saath na gine jaayein.
  • Ek depot doosre ka order queue na dekh sake. Do login isi liye bante hain — ek se ye test nahi hota.

Part 7 — Claim aur incentive

  • Ek hi claim do baar approve karne pe doosra credit note na bane.
  • Incentive — target achievement se bane; rep ke phone pe My performance me wahi number dikhe jo Desk me hai.

Bug kaise likhein

Jo bhi "Ye hona chahiye" galat nikle, usme ye chaar cheezein likhein — inke bina koi bug theek nahi hota:

  1. Kahan — phone, Desk ya portal; kaunsi screen; kis login se.
  2. Kya kiya — kadam-ba-kadam, jaise aapne kiya.
  3. Kya hona chahiye tha aur kya hua. Number ho to dono number likhein.
  4. Kaunsa record — order number, shop ka naam, receipt number. Screenshot sabse accha.

Sabse kaam ka bug report: "Nagpal Complex pe cart ₹981.80 bola, Desk me Sales Order ₹1,099.62 nikla, order FM-ORD-000169, rep test@rep.com." Ye seedha wajah tak le jaata hai. "Order kaam nahi kar raha" se kuch nahi hota.
